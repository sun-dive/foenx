Contact

foen is made by sun-dive. There is no company office and no call centre; there is a person, and a public repository.



There is no email address here on purpose: a published address is a spam magnet. Messages go straight to the operator and nowhere else.

Problems with the software
Open an issue at github.com/sun-dive/foenx/issues. That is where fixes are tracked, and anyone can read what has been reported.

Advertising and business
Use the form. Direct placements on the idle screen are available to businesses whose products suit a free telephone.

Before you write

  • We cannot recover a number. It is made from the twelve words on your own device and never reaches us. The help page explains how to restore from the words.
  • We cannot tell you who called you or whom a number called. The relay keeps no logs; see the privacy page.
  • To report abuse, say what happened and when. The relay limits abusive behaviour as it occurs; it cannot look back.
The page and the relay are open source: github.com/sun-dive/foenx.
