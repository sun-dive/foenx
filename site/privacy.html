Privacy

Last updated 16 September 2026. This page describes what actually happens, in plain words. Where a fact changes, this page changes with it.

There is no account

foen does not ask for a name, an email address, a phone number or a password, and it has no way to identify you. Your foen number is made on your own device from your twelve recovery words. We never see the words, and we cannot recover a number for you.

What stays on your device

Your browser keeps, for this site only:

  • the key that signs your calls, held by the browser in a form that no web page, including ours, can read;
  • your number, and the name you typed if you listed one;
  • the last person you called and the calls you have answered or declined, so the page can offer a call-back button and not ring twice for the same call;
  • your choice of look and of camera and microphone.

Clearing the site's data in your browser removes all of it. Your number can then be restored only from your words.

What passes through the relay

A call travels from your device to our relay and on to the other person's device, over an encrypted connection to the relay on each side. The relay holds the last few seconds of each call, a rolling window of about six seconds per direction, and overwrites it continuously. It is not recorded, and what remains of the window is deleted within an hour of the call ending. Every fragment is signed by the sender's key, so the other side can tell it came from you and not from us or anyone in between.

Be aware of what this does and does not mean: the relay could in principle see a call as it passes, in the same way a telephone exchange could. It does not record, and we run no software that looks. Protection of the content from the relay itself is planned and will be described here when it exists.

What the relay stores

  • The phone book, if you choose to list yourself: your name and your number, signed by your key, kept for thirty days after the last time you listed or refreshed it, then deleted. Listing is optional and off by default. Anyone can read the phone book; that is its purpose.
  • Invitations: when someone calls you, a short signed note saying who is calling waits for your page to pick it up. It is overwritten by later invitations and deleted within an hour in any case.
  • Nothing else. The relay keeps no access logs: no addresses, no times, no record of who called whom. The web server is configured without an access log. We cannot answer "who did this number call" because nothing records it.

Cookies

foen itself sets no cookies. It uses the browser's own storage for the items listed above, which never leave your device.

Advertising

foen is free and is paid for by advertising shown on the idle screen and after a call, never during one. When an advertising network's banners run on the page, that network may set its own cookies and collect its own data under its own policy; where the law requires it, you are asked for consent first. The page in the plain look, made for tablets set up for someone else, carries no advertising.

Children

foen is a telephone. It collects nothing from anyone, and it has no features aimed at children.

Changes

If what the service does changes, this page is updated and the date at the top moves. The page and the relay software are public, so what is written here can be checked against what runs.
